Who we are

Campus navigation, mapped by the people who walk it.

UCampass is a web-based university map. It covers lecture halls, labs, walkways and bus stops, and it gives you walking directions from one room to the next.

No app to installOpen it in any browser, or scan the QR code at the campus gates.

The problem

Big campuses are hard to get around

University campuses work like small townships, but the maps people rely on stop at the front gate.

Scenario

Students, staff and visitors lose time getting lost, then drive 500 m trips to escape the tropical heat. That adds carbon emissions and traffic inside the campus.

1

No pedestrian maps

Google Maps misses internal footpaths and covered walkways, so lost visitors and late students are the norm.

2

Complex layouts

Multi-building campuses and changing class venues make every commute feel like a navigation puzzle.

3

Car over foot

Short trips by car or motorbike add greenhouse gas emissions and congestion to a space built for people.

Our solution

One map for the whole campus

A lightweight, web-based Uni Map: open it in any browser or scan a QR code at campus gates. No app download.

Interactive campus map

Complete indoor-to-outdoor mapping of lecture halls, facilities and bus stops on any device.

Intelligent auto-routing

Finds the best walking route or builds a whole-semester itinerary from a timetable.

Secure user login

Personalised features and role-based views for students versus visitors.
